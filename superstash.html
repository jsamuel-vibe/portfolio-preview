Superstash: a personal knowledge library for designers

I designed and built a place to collect references and notes, with a longer-term goal: giving AI tools useful context from what I save, learn and think.

Visit Superstash website

Long story short

My references and thoughts were scattered across different apps. I kept collecting things to learn from, but often struggled to bring that knowledge back into the work I was doing.

I wanted Superstash to connect that collection with AI tools, helping resurface forgotten references and inform new ideas. Working solo, I started with the foundation: browser capture, a shared library for saves and notes, and AI-generated descriptions and tags.

Using it myself made quick note-taking a priority. I chose a simpler interface that I could build sooner, with a note field directly in the gallery and compact navigation.

The API connection remains planned. My immediate focus is fixing bugs and refining the existing UI and workflows before expanding the AI capabilities.

What I’ve built today

References and thinking, in one workspace

The working product brings saved references and notes into a shared library. Browser capture collects material from the web, while a quick-note field in the gallery makes room for the thoughts, critiques and ideas that come with it.

Context without organising everything by hand

AI-generated descriptions and tags add context to saved material. This is the first part of the larger ambition: building a collection that could eventually give an AI tool useful context about my interests and thinking.
